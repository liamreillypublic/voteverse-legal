1. Introduction

VoteVerse ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using VoteVerse, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use the App.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of VoteVerse, regardless of how you access or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE DIRECTLY:

  • Email address (for account creation and authentication)
  • Display name (publicly visible to other users)
  • Password (encrypted via Firebase Authentication — we never store plaintext passwords)
  • User-generated vote content (custom vote questions you create)
  • Friend connections (users you add as friends)

INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY:

  • Voting activity (which options you vote for, when you vote)
  • Prediction data (your predictions and their outcomes)
  • Game statistics (streaks, points, gems, leaderboard position)
  • In-app purchase history (what you purchased, not payment details)
  • Push notification token (for delivering notifications to your device)
  • App usage data (session timestamps, feature interactions)
  • Device type and operating system version

INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT:

  • Your real name (unless you choose it as your display name)
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your full contacts or address book — the "Find Friends" feature extracts only email addresses from your contacts and checks them against our user database. No contact names, phone numbers, or other data are transmitted. Email addresses are used solely for matching and are not stored on our servers.
  • Your payment information (handled entirely by Apple)
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Browsing history or data from other apps
  • Biometric data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To provide the core VoteVerse experience (voting, predictions, streaks, leaderboards)
  • To display your profile, display name, and stats to other users
  • To enable friend connections and multiplayer friend games
  • To process and track in-app purchases and virtual currency (gems)
  • To send push notifications about game activity, friend requests, streak reminders, and other app-related events
  • To moderate user-generated content for safety and compliance
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Use
  • To maintain, improve, and optimize the App's performance and features
  • To enforce our Terms of Use
  • To respond to your requests or inquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do NOT use your information for:

  • Selling to third parties
  • Targeted advertising based on personal data (ads shown are not personalized)
  • Profiling for purposes unrelated to the App

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Data is stored on servers located in the United States.

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and salted (via Firebase Authentication) — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Firestore Security Rules restrict data access on a per-user basis
  • Cloud Functions validate all scoring, purchases, and sensitive operations server-side
  • Rate limiting on authentication attempts to prevent brute-force attacks
  • Automated content moderation on user-generated content
  • Regular review of security practices

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide you with the App's services.

Upon account deletion:

  • Your profile, display name, and email are permanently deleted
  • Your voting history, predictions, and game stats are permanently deleted
  • Your friend connections are removed
  • Your gems and in-app purchase entitlements are forfeited (no refunds)
  • Aggregated, anonymized data (e.g., total vote counts on rounds) may be retained as it is no longer personally identifiable

We may also retain certain data as required by law, to resolve disputes, enforce our Terms of Use, or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention).

Inactive accounts may be subject to deletion after an extended period of inactivity, with prior notice sent to the registered email address.

9. Subscriptions & Purchase Data

If you subscribe to VoteVerse VIP or make in-app purchases, the following applies:

  • All payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information (credit card, billing address, etc.).
  • We receive and store: your subscription status (active/expired), purchase history (product IDs), and transaction identifiers for verification purposes.
  • VIP subscriptions auto-renew weekly at the current price until canceled. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings at any time.
  • Subscription status is synced to our servers to unlock premium features (ad removal, friend games, weekly gem rewards).

10. Push Notifications

VoteVerse uses local and/or push notifications to inform you about:

You can control notifications through:

Disabling notifications will not affect your ability to use the App, but you may miss time-sensitive information such as streak expiry warnings.
